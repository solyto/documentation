---

The Feeds feature is a full RSS/Atom news reader. Add any feed URL and read your news without leaving solyto.

## Adding feeds

Paste any RSS or Atom feed URL to subscribe. solyto fetches new items in the background.

## Reading

- Unread items are shown first
- Mark items as read individually or all at once
- Open the original article in a new tab
- Save items to your [Link Library](/libraries/links/)

## Organizing feeds

Group feeds into categories. Filter your reading view by category.

## Exporting

Your feed subscriptions can be exported as an `.opml` file, which imports into any feed reader (Feedly, NewsBlur, etc.). See [Exporting Data](/integrations/exporting/).
