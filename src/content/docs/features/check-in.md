Check In lets you record a daily entry about how you're doing — mood, health metrics, notes, and more. Over time it builds a personal log you can look back on.

## What you can track

Each check-in entry can include:

- **Mood** — a score or emoji-based rating
- **Health** — custom health metrics (sleep, energy, etc.)
- **Notes** — free-text notes about your day
- **Tags** — categorize entries for filtering

## Viewing history

Browse past entries in a calendar or list view. Filter by date range or tag.

## Trends

solyto shows simple trend charts so you can see patterns in your mood and health data over time.

## Exporting

Check-in data exports as `.csv`. See [Exporting Data](/integrations/exporting/).
