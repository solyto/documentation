---
title: Finances
description: Track income and wealth over time.
---

The Finances feature has two sub-sections: **Income** for tracking earnings and expenses, and **Wealth** for tracking your net worth over time.

## Income

Log income and expense entries:

- Amount, date, and description
- Category (salary, freelance, rent, groceries, etc.)
- Recurring entries for regular income/expenses

View totals by category and date range. See a monthly breakdown of what came in and went out.

## Wealth

Track your net worth by logging snapshots of your assets and liabilities:

- Bank accounts
- Investments
- Real estate
- Debts and loans

solyto shows a chart of your net worth over time.

## Exporting

Both Income and Wealth data export as `.csv` files, compatible with spreadsheet tools. See [Exporting Data](/integrations/exporting/).
