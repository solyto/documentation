Topics are focused learning modules built into solyto. Each topic covers a specific subject in depth as an interactive reference you can use alongside your practice. They are self-contained modules that require no setup or data entry — just open and explore.

Topics live inside solyto alongside your other features, so you don't need to install anything extra or switch to a separate app.

## Available topics

### Guitar Theory

An interactive guitar theory reference, accessible at `/topics/guitar-theory`. It covers the fundamentals every guitar player needs:

- **Notes on the fretboard** — visualize every note on each string and fret
- **Scales and modes** — see scale patterns and fingerings for major, minor, pentatonic, blues, and more
- **Chord shapes and inversions** — common chord voicings across the neck, from open chords to barre chords
- **Intervals and harmony** — understand the relationships between notes and how chords are built

All diagrams are rendered in the browser and respond to clicks and taps. You can highlight individual notes, show scale positions across the entire fretboard, and switch between different chord voicings.

The module includes interactive fretboard diagrams you can explore visually, making it easy to find notes, chords, and scales anywhere on the neck. Tap or click on the fretboard to see note names, highlight scale positions, or display chord fingerings.

The Guitar Theory topic is designed for players of all levels — whether you're learning your first open chords or exploring advanced modes.

## Using Topics

Topics are self-contained reference modules — you don't need to enter any data or set anything up. Open a topic and use it as a reference while practicing or studying. They work on both desktop and mobile, so you can keep a topic open on your phone while playing.

### How to access

Navigate directly to a topic's route. For example, the Guitar Theory topic is available at `/topics/guitar-theory`.

You can also find Topics in the main navigation menu under the Features section.
