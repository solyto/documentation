title: Clipboard
description: Copy text across devices using solyto as a relay.
---

The Clipboard feature is a cross-device clipboard relay. Copy something on your phone, read it on your desktop — without sending it through a third-party service.

## How it works

1. Paste or type text into the Clipboard in solyto on one device
2. Open solyto on another device — the text is there

Clipboard entries are stored in your solyto account, so they're only as persistent as you want. Delete them when you're done.

## Use cases

- Copy a link from your phone to paste on your desktop
- Move a code snippet between machines
- Share text between a browser and a terminal

## Privacy

Clipboard content is stored in your solyto account — on solyto.app, or on your own server if self-hosting. It is never processed or read by solyto.
