The dashboard is the first thing you see when you open solyto. It pulls together data from across your features into one view so you know what your day looks like at a glance.

## Sections

### Your Day (left column)

Shows your agenda for today:

- Upcoming todos due today or overdue
- Calendar events for today
- Current weather
- Active time tracking session (if running)

### Inspiration (center column)

A rotating piece of content to start your day:

- A random quote from your Quote Library
- A book or album recommendation

### Quick Glance (right column)

A compact summary of the rest of your data:

- Unread feed items
- Recent check-in entry
- Active pomodoro timer

## Onboarding

On your first visit, solyto shows an onboarding modal to help you get set up, followed by a guided welcome tour that walks through the main features. You can restart the tour from **Settings → General**.

## Customizing the dashboard

You can control which features appear in the dashboard by toggling features on and off in **Settings → Features**. Disabled features are hidden from the dashboard and the navigation.
