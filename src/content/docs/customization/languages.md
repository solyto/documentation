solyto supports four interface languages. Change your language in **Settings → Localization**.

## Changing language

1. Go to **Settings → Localization**
2. Select your preferred language from the **Language** dropdown
3. The interface updates immediately

## Available languages

- **English** (en)
- **German** (de)
- **French** (fr)
- **Spanish** (es)

The language is chosen at registration and auto-detected from your browser otherwise. The Telegram bot and other integrations follow your account language setting.

## Contributing translations

If a translation is missing or incomplete, contributions are welcome. Translation files live in the [app repository](https://github.com/solyto/app) under `src/lib/i18n/`.
