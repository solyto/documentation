## What's in solyto?

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Libraries">
		Track your books, music, movies, games, recipes, links, quotes, and plants. Your collection, organized your way — with search, ratings, genres, and wishlists.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Todos">
		Tasks, projects, deadlines. Check things off, feel good about it. No gamification, no streaks guilt-tripping you.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Calendar">
		Your schedule without the bloat. CalDAV sync if you need it. No "smart" suggestions, no meeting spam.
	</Card>
	<Card title="News">
		RSS feeds from sources you choose. No algorithm deciding what you should read.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Notebook">
		Notes and thoughts. Write things down, organize them, find them later.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Contacts">
		Your address book, synced across devices. CardDAV compatible, privacy-first.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Finance">
		Track expenses and income. Simple budgeting — no complexity, no selling your financial data.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Clipboard">
		Copy on your phone, paste on your desktop. A shared clipboard across all your devices — no cables, no AirDrop, no workarounds.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Daily Check-In">
		Reflect on your day. Mood tracking, journaling, and habit building in one place.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Time Tracker">
		Know where your time goes. Track projects, billable hours, and productivity without the stress.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
