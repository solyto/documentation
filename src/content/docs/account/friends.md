The Friends feature lets you connect with other solyto users and share calendars with them.

## How it works

Friends is a mutual connection system. Both users must agree to the connection before any sharing takes place. You control who you connect with and what you share.

## Sending a friend request

1. Go to **Settings → Friends** or the Friends section
2. Search for the person by their email address
3. Click **Send Request**

The other user receives a notification and can accept or reject the request.

## Managing requests

- **Incoming requests** — view pending requests and accept or reject them
- **Outgoing requests** — see requests you've sent that haven't been answered yet
- **Cancel** — you can cancel a sent request at any time before it's accepted

## Friends list

Your friends list shows all accepted connections. From here you can:

- View a friend's profile
- Remove a friend (this also removes any calendar sharing between you)
- Manage calendar sharing

## Calendar sharing

Once connected, you can share [Calendars](/features/calendar/) with friends:

1. Open **Calendar settings**
2. Select a calendar to share
3. Choose which friends to share it with
4. Set permissions (view-only or view-and-edit)

Shared calendars appear in your friend's calendar view alongside their own. Changes sync in real time.

Only the calendars you explicitly share are visible to your friends. Everything else remains private.

## Privacy

- Your data is never visible to friends unless you explicitly share it
- Shared calendars can be revoked at any time
- Removing a friend removes all sharing between you
- Friend connections are private — your friends list is not visible to other users

## Self-hosting note

Friends works across accounts on the same solyto instance. If you self-host, users on your instance can connect with each other. Cross-instance friends are not supported.
