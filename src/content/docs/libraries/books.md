title: Book Library

The Book Library tracks books you've read, are currently reading, or want to read — your personal reading log.

## Adding books

Add entries manually or import from:

- **Goodreads** — import your shelves (CSV export from Goodreads)
- **Hardcover** — import your reading history

See [Importing Data](/integrations/importing/) for details.

## Entry fields

Each book entry includes:

- Title, author, and ISBN
- Year and genre
- Status (read, reading, want to read, DNF)
- Rating (1–5)
- Start and finish dates
- Tags and notes

## Recommendations

solyto can suggest books based on your library — either more from authors you've read, or new authors in genres you like.

## Release notifications

Follow an author to be notified when they publish a new book. See [Notifications](/customization/notifications/).

## Exporting

Exports as `.csv` in the Goodreads format, making it easy to move your data elsewhere. See [Exporting Data](/integrations/exporting/).
