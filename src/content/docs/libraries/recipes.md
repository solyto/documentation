The Recipe Library is where you collect recipes you've tried or want to try.

## Entry fields

- Title, source, and cuisine
- Ingredients list
- Step-by-step instructions
- Prep time, cook time, and servings
- Rating (1–5)
- Tags (e.g. vegetarian, quick, weekend)
- Notes

## Exporting

Exports as `.csv` with ingredients and steps flattened. See [Exporting Data](/integrations/exporting/).
