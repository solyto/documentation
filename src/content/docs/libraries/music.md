The Music Library is where you catalogue the music you own and love — albums, artists, and your listening history.

## Adding music

Add entries manually or import from:

- **Deezer** — import your favorites and library
- **Discogs** — import your vinyl/CD collection

See [Importing Data](/integrations/importing/) for details.

## Entry fields

Each album entry includes:

- Title and artist
- Year and genre
- Format (vinyl, CD, digital, streaming)
- Rating (1–5)
- Status (owned, wishlist, sold)
- Tags and notes

## Recommendations

solyto can recommend albums based on your library. Click **Recommendations** to get a list of releases you might like — either from artists you already have, or new ones.

## Release notifications

Subscribe to an artist to get notified when they release new music. Notifications can be sent via Telegram, email, or push. See [Notifications](/customization/notifications/).

## Exporting

Exports as `.csv`. See [Exporting Data](/integrations/exporting/).
