solyto includes a suite of personal libraries for tracking the things you own, read, watch, play, cook, and collect. All libraries share a consistent interface: add entries, tag them, search them, and export them.

## Available libraries

| Library | What you track |
|---------|---------------|
| [Music Library](/libraries/music/) | Albums and artists you own or love |
| [Book Library](/libraries/books/) | Books you've read, are reading, or want to read |
| [Movie Library](/libraries/movies/) | Films you've watched or want to watch |
| [Game Library](/libraries/games/) | Video games you own or have played |
| [Recipe Library](/libraries/recipes/) | Recipes you've collected |
| [Quote Library](/libraries/quotes/) | Quotes that meant something to you |
| [Link Library](/libraries/links/) | Bookmarks and saved URLs |
| [Plant Library](/libraries/plants/) | Your houseplants and garden |

## Common features

All libraries support:

- **Tags** — organize entries into categories
- **Search** — full-text search across all entries
- **Ratings** — rate entries on a 1–5 scale
- **Status** — mark entries as owned, wanted, completed, etc.
- **Notes** — add free-text notes to any entry
- **Export** — download your data as CSV

## Recommendations

The Music and Book libraries can show you recommendations based on what you already have. These are generated on-demand and do not send your data anywhere.

## Release notifications

For artists in your Music Library and authors in your Book Library, you can opt in to receive notifications when they release new music or books. See [Notifications](/customization/notifications/).
