The Quote Library is a personal collection of quotes — from books, people, films, or wherever you encounter them.

## Entry fields

- Quote text
- Author
- Source (book title, film, etc.)
- Tags
- Notes

## Dashboard

A random quote from your library can appear on your dashboard under the Inspiration section each day.

## Exporting

Exports as `.csv` with text, author, and source. See [Exporting Data](/integrations/exporting/).
