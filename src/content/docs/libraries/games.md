---
title: Game Library
description: Track video games you own or have played.
---

The Game Library tracks video games — what you own, what you're playing, what you've completed, and what you want to play.

## Entry fields

- Title, platform, and genre
- Status (owned, playing, completed, wishlist, dropped)
- Rating (1–5)
- Tags and notes

## Exporting

Exports as `.csv`. See [Exporting Data](/integrations/exporting/).
