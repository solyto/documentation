title: Plant Library
description: Track your houseplants and garden.
---

The Plant Library is where you catalogue your houseplants, garden plants, and outdoor plants.

## Entry fields

- Common name and scientific name
- Location (room, garden bed, etc.)
- Acquisition date
- Watering schedule
- Notes on care and health

## Reminders

Set watering and fertilizing reminders. Notifications can be sent via Telegram, email, or push. See [Notifications](/customization/notifications/).
