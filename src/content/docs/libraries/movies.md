The Movie Library is your personal film log. Track movies you've watched, rate them, and maintain a watchlist.

## Adding movies

Add entries manually. Search by title to auto-fill metadata.

## Entry fields

- Title, year, director, and genre
- Status (watched, want to watch)
- Rating (1–5)
- Tags and notes

## Exporting

Exports as `.csv`. See [Exporting Data](/integrations/exporting/).
