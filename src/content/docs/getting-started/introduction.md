solyto is a free, private, all-in-one personal management app. It covers your todos, contacts, calendar, notes, news, libraries, finances, and more — in one place, with one login, and one coherent interface.

## Why solyto?

Most personal productivity tools share the same problems:

- **Fragmentation** — you need five apps to do what one should do
- **Bloat** — tools keep adding AI features, analytics, and subscriptions you never asked for
- **Privacy** — your data is used to train models or sold to advertisers
- **Cost** — essential features are paywalled

solyto was built out of frustration with all of the above. It does not track you, does not contact external services without your knowledge, and does not charge you.

## Core principles

- **No tracking** — no cookies, no analytics, no telemetry
- **No AI** — your data is yours, not a training set
- **No subscriptions** — free to use, free to self-host
- **No bloat** — features you asked for, nothing else

## How to use solyto

You have three options:

1. **solyto.app** — use the hosted version at [solyto.app](https://solyto.app)
2. **Local Docker** — run it locally via the Docker Compose stack
3. **Self-host** — deploy it on your own server using our Docker images

See [Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start/) or [Self-Hosting](/getting-started/self-hosting/) for setup instructions.
